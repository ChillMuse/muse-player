What is Muse?+

Muse is a music player for macOS. Home lets you pick up where you left off and browse recently added albums. Library organizes your albums, artists, and songs. Radio starts from a record or artist.

How do I browse my library?+

Switch between Albums, Artists, and Songs, or use Search the library to find your music. Albums shows cover artwork, Artists groups music by artist, and Songs lists individual tracks.

How does Radio work?+

Pick a record to start from. Radio keeps going from there, following how the music sounds and what the songs are about. Skipping a track steers it away from songs like that one.

How do I add music?+

Search for a song or an album. It downloads in the background and appears in the library when it’s ready. Search by song, album, or artist, and follow the progress in Downloading.

Where can I download Muse?+

Muse is a macOS-only app. The installer is not available yet. When it is ready, you will find the download and installation instructions on GitHub Releases.
