What is Muse?

Muse is a music player for macOS and Windows. It plays the music folders on your computer, with no account and no server: it runs only on your computer. Home lets you pick up where you left off and browse recently added albums. Library organizes your albums, artists, and songs. Muse starts from a record or artist.

How do I browse my library?

Switch between Albums, Artists, and Songs, or use Search the library to find your music. Albums shows cover artwork, Artists groups music by artist, and Songs lists individual tracks.

How does Muse work?

Pick a record to start from. Muse keeps going from there, following how the music sounds and what the songs are about. Skipping a track steers it away from songs like that one. To follow the sound and lyrics, install smart features in Settings. They are not available on Intel Macs.

Where can I download Muse?

Muse is available for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows. Choose your installer in Get Muse or on GitHub Releases. Intel Macs get the app without smart features.

Why does my computer warn me about Muse?

The installers are not signed with an Apple or Microsoft developer certificate yet, so macOS and Windows show a warning the first time you open Muse. The warning means the developer is not verified, not that something harmful was found. Muse contains no viruses or malware: its source code is public on GitHub, and the installers are built from it by GitHub Actions.
