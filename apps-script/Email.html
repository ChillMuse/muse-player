THE HIDDEN FEATURE

Find it.
Add music.

Thanks for signing up, . Here’s the feature we keep off the website: Muse can search for a song or an album, download it in the background and add it to your library. It stays hidden until you unlock it.

01 Open Muse and choose Settings in the sidebar.
02 Press the secret key.
macOSCtrl + Shift + Option + D
WindowsCtrl + Shift + Alt + D
03 In the Administrator password box, enter the password.
download-enjoy

Add appears in the sidebar. Search by song, album or artist, and follow the progress in Downloading.

WHILE YOU’RE IN SETTINGS

Install Smart features too, so Muse can choose better next songs. In Settings › Smart features, click Install. It’s a one-time download of about 3.6 GB, and everything runs on your computer.

Smart features need a Mac with Apple silicon or Windows.
